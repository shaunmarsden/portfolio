JUDGEMENT, NOT JUST OUTPUT

The work I want
to be useful for.

01 / Frame

Choose the simplest useful approach.

Compare AI with deterministic rules and a credible manual alternative. Define success and stopping criteria before the result is known.

02 / Challenge

Make consequential failures visible.

Test missing evidence, repeated commands, weak permissions and partial failures. Keep severity separate from aggregate scores.

03 / Integrate

Keep authority at the boundary.

Use validation, approval states, durable intent and receipts. Treat a recommendation as input to a decision, not permission to act.

These capabilities are illustrated by the artefacts. They are not an independently assessed proficiency rating or a claim of workplace outcomes.
